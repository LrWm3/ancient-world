# Matched employer and household-income experiments

These are game-balance checks, not empirical wage or business-survival calibration. Modes share seeds, catalogs, geography and history duration. Later population differences include feedback; controlled fixtures identify the immediate cash and capacity mediators.

Closure counts come from persistent firm records, not annual snapshots. Five-year survival uses only firms founded at least five years before the end; still-active younger firms are censored. Accounts, closure reasons and eligible counts are retained in summary.json and raw trajectories. No runtime here is an isolated hardware benchmark.
