# Continuing institutional study pilot

`Culture.continuing_students` is an opt-in request-selection policy. With
`institutional_students` also enabled, one quarter in four favors an eligible
student's greatest unfinished progress in a topic that a present member of an
operational local institution can teach. Another quarter retains rotating student
admission; the other two retain general cultural actor selection. Ties retain the
ordinary rotation. Completing a topic removes its priority. A dead, absent or
unqualified teacher cannot be replaced by the institution's historical topic list.

With the policy enabled, institutional source selection also prefers unfinished
teachable topics over starting the lowest-numbered new topic. It searches eligible
local institutions; it does not create knowledge or promise continued attendance.
The student's earlier source event is retained by the existing learning system.
Existing manuscript-first study selection remains in force: a readable manuscript
can still determine the actual requested study instead of the institutional offer.

Selection reads the Reserve boundary. The dated work plan captures policy, actor,
source, opening progress and expected gain. Actual personal time, writing supplies,
room capacity and funding still determine execution. No extra lesson rate, labor,
materials or progress is granted. The policy is not permanent enrollment and does
not prevent later closure, illness or departure from interrupting study.

The evaluation runner exposes `--continuing-students`, requiring
`--institutional-students`, and records both flags. Old archives and plans default
the policy to disabled/unspecified. Defaults remain unchanged pending comparison.

## Verification and evaluation

The institutional source fixture checks competing students, unfinished versus new
topics, absent topic sources, mastered topics, alternating admission/general
turns, unchanged progress during planning, no automatic labor grant, and serialized
plan reproduction. Results and matched balance evidence will be recorded after
execution; this document does not claim that more focused requests improve final
knowledge or population.

The source/selection fixture passes on Vulkan. The frozen monthly/batch/checkpoint
fixture also passes seeds 17/81/256 (policy enabled in 17/256, legacy control in
81). Ordinary library tests pass 137 cases, with 117 hardware/extended cases skipped;
all-target Clippy passes. A subsequent lookup refactor reuses the already observed
present-person list during actor selection instead of rebuilding it for each
candidate; its source/selection fixture also passes.

Matched thirty-year runs use seeds 17/81/256, terrain 32/ecology 16, one epoch,
yield 0.5, individual demography and workshop/agriculture/extraction/construction
refinement. Both arms enable named institution administration, operating budgets,
essential-first work, student selection and working-core space. Family support is
off. Only `--continuing-students` differs. The runner now separately records
institutional requests, requests with unfinished progress, actual progress,
progressing lessons and acquired topics; manuscript study is excluded from these
institutional counters. Reports stay under `output/continuing-study-{control,enabled}.json`.
