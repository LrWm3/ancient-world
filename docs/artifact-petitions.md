# Local artifact petitions

`Generator::petition_artifact` queues an existing claimant's request at the
object's local office. Supported remedies are negotiated return, compensation
paid by an institutional owner, and an explicitly unresolved case. The original
owner, claimant, jurisdiction, proposed terms and consents are recorded.

Respond hears at most one case per office per month, using 0.05 months within
already-completed named office service. Without service the case waits, expiring
unresolved after twelve months. Requests made at a completed boundary cannot use
that same month's work. This is a bounded use of office attendance, not an extra
labor grant. It does not yet allocate hearing time against individual tax cases.

A settlement requires current access and consent of the owner, custodian and
receiving claimant. Return of title additionally requires all recorded claimants
to agree. Return changes title and custody, with continuous object
provenance. Compensation consumes the owner's real treasury and credits the
claimant's existing institution, community or household account. Insufficient funds, changed
control, lost access or missing agreement leave title and claims intact. Money is
never minted to close a case. This first version negotiates disputes; it does not
infer criminal guilt or authorize unilateral confiscation.

The petition, outcome, actual payment and causal events persist in governance.
An ordinary title sale remains blocked while unresolved claims exist.

## Claimant-specific settlement refinement

Paid compensation now credits the claimant's existing account: the institution's
own treasury, the community's municipal account, or the person's current household
wallet. A person without a locally represented household/account retains an
unresolved claim; no account or money is invented. The petition records the
actual receiving household ID so later relocation does not obscure where payment
went. The receiving balance's representable increment is the payer's debit.

Payment releases only that claimant's interest. Other claims remain attached to
the artifact and continue blocking ordinary sale. Returning title still requires
all claimants' agreement; buying out one claim does not extinguish unrelated rights.

Household cash reconciliation includes `legal_compensation_received`, separate
from wages, dividends and relief. This is a transfer receipt, not new income
created by the office. Older accounts default to zero recorded compensation.
