# Patron foundings and living traditions

New histories now start with named guides accompanying the initial human communities from the Ancient World. The voyage is a recorded prologue; settlements and daughter towns remain on the inner continents. Existing histories receive an explicitly identified cultural baseline, without invented witnesses or retroactive patrons.

## Running and inspecting

The normal founding call and desktop founding controls use this system. The sidebar exposes service duration and an aid control before founding; the history window has separate towns/economy, founding culture and chronicle tabs. Cultural inspection separates recorded arrivals/departures from attributed religious accounts, and factual links open the chronicle. Settlement inspection includes crops, herds, institutions, objects, and production roles. Atlas settlement markers can show political control, religious affiliation, or economic role.

```sh
cargo run --release --example history_evaluate -- \
  --seeds 17,81,256,409,1024 --years 200 --discoveries --living-world \
  --save-worlds --output output/patron-complete
cargo run --release --example history_evaluate -- \
  --seeds 17,81 --years 500 --discoveries --living-world \
  --save-worlds --output output/patron-complete-long
```

Use `--no-patron-aid` for the matched assistance control and `--legacy-farming` for the scalar-farm comparison. Starting supplies remain identical under the aid control. A legacy-farming comparison retains declared seeds and animals in storage rather than deleting their inventory. Existing island nutrient, plot-area, and yield settings remain the baseline.

Reusable APIs include `found_civilizations_with_options`, `found_civilizations_with_catalog`, `cultural_history`, `set_diversified_farming`, `dedicate_artifact`, `sell_artifact`, and `destroy_artifact`. The history exposes `cultural_summary` and `settlement_roles`; its cultural records use stable numeric references. Headless founding accepts `--patron-catalog assets/patrons.toml` and `--no-patron-aid`.

## Mechanics and accounting

- `assets/patrons.toml` contains eight semi-intelligent animals and four intelligent beings. Appearance, communication, ecological origin preference, teaching, and aid strength are editable. Default service is 84 or 36 months, scaled by seeded variance and divided by `1 + aid_strength`.
- Each patron has an actual Ancient World origin, a coastal island landing connected to its settlement, a schematic voyage, founding witnesses, intended departure, delivered effort, and a separate finite sustenance ledger. Patrons depart even when their town has failed. Their mandate remains unknown.
- Assistance trains existing people, makes construction materials from existing stocks, and reduces existing raid risks. It creates no money, food, or fertility. Learned knowledge persists after departure. Effort is recorded by activity; scouting records nearby surveyed cells in a living witness’s place knowledge and improves navigation skill. Service includes its final month, and later offerings follow the actual departure anniversary.
- Traditions begin with four independently drawn human themes. A patron may weakly influence one theme. Accounts cite factual events and their human transmitters. Household affiliation survives changes in political administration. Contact, hardship, leadership and sustained support can produce adoption, reinterpretation, syncretism, and schism while retaining patron ancestry.
- Six disposition axes and skills extend existing historical people. Quarterly actions use locally present adults, available materials, money and reserved craft labor. Named people teach, organize institutions, sponsor charitable transfers, make manuscripts or objects, undertake local pilgrimages, seek office, and sometimes steal objects. These activities share a finite quarterly work allowance. Succession considers personal ambition and loyalty.
- Religious orders, merchant houses, craft guilds and scholarly circles have treasuries, members, succession, knowledge and property. Contributions transfer community money; upkeep pays money back into the local economy. Merchant sponsorship uses the existing expedition escrow and refund path.
- Objects distinguish physical custody from ownership and competing claims. Creation removes ordinary materials from market inventory into the object; destruction returns embodied matter to managed detritus/waste once. Foundations hold their building material. Manuscripts require writing knowledge and writing materials. Later-born people start without automatic knowledge and learn through teachers, readable objects and member institutions. Knowledge records link learning events to teachers and source manuscripts. Manuscript authors prefer topics not already preserved locally. Death transfers custody rather than duplicating objects, and does not confer title to a borrowed or stolen object.

`assets/agriculture.toml` defines six crops and three livestock groups. GPU production tracks standing crops, seeds, harvests, living herds, feed, births, deaths, products, and fish withdrawals. Crops share limited land and labor and require suitable temperature, rainfall, water and nutrients. Livestock consumes real feed and returns residual nutrients. Fisheries withdraw from a particular neighboring lake ecology cell, with a stable withdrawal order for competing towns. Crafting uses finite material recipes and reserved labor; advanced recipes require local knowledge. Raw food storage and spoilage are bounded, as is the existing granary.

The economy has 64 GPU goods slots and up to 64 recipes. The first eight material identities remain compatible with old saves; the final slot represents the existing aggregate dietary food stock. Raw foods have distinct energy and C/N/P. Converting them into dietary stock is constrained by both calories and embodied elements; leftovers become compost. This is an explicit regional dietary abstraction, not detailed meal simulation. Settlement roles update annually from recent activity, with an 80% carryover of previous role scores. Production investments, knowledge, institutions and existing infrastructure constrain changes in specialization. Tools wear into finite scrap: recovery and fuelled remelting each retain 90%, with the remainder recorded as loss. Agricultural nitrogen fixation draws from the production energy budget; the editable `fixation_cost_kg` is 12 for new worlds and defaults to the historical 80 when absent in an archived catalog.

## Persistence and limits

Archive header version three stores catalogs, crop/herd inventories, patron schedules, accounts, affiliations, people, institutions, objects and knowledge at the existing consistent history boundary. Version-two archive loading pads old eight-good arrays, translates the old food-cargo identity, and introduces a marked cultural baseline. Terrain and ecological state continue through the existing versioned format.

Patron journeys and service are represented at the scale of the founding history. Patron sustenance has its own declared import/consumption/return ledger outside managed farm plots. Cultural spending is community/institutional rather than a separate wallet for every person. Object custody, inheritance, theft, dedication, local negotiated sales and destruction work. Sales use the existing institutional and communal accounts, reject disputed title and insufficient funds, and keep objects at their physical site. Pilgrimages require an open physical route, provisions, an offering and reserved work; only round trips that fit within the monthly regional travel budget execute. Office campaigns require personal supporters, available funds and reserved labor, and feed into existing faction leadership rules. Curating an expedition specimen transfers a finite sample from delivered workshop stocks into unique custody without importing its elements again. Abandoned objects remain in ruins until local recovery; lost manuscripts cannot teach. Further encounters and the Ancient World remain open areas for development.

## Validation and calibration

The evaluator exports cultural counts, per-town farming and role summaries, C/N/P/water/money/goods residuals, yearly population and shortages, expeditions, and stage timings. Hardware tests cover finite aid, reliable departures, saved continuation across different batch sizes, aid-control inventories, invalid references, material destruction, crop resource limits, livestock/food accounting, markets and ecology. Additional controlled fixtures exercise annual role inertia, pilgrimage travel and provisions, artifact loss/recovery, unique specimen accounting, office campaign spending, schism ancestry and institutional expedition escrow/refund. The accompanying results record the final integrated test outcome. See the accompanying results for measured runs rather than assuming every religion divides or every expedition succeeds.

Early agricultural calibrations caused widespread collapse. Two causes were corrected: already climate-adjusted survey yields were being climate-limited a second time, and a three-month processed-food target prevented the twelve-month provisions required for daughter towns and expeditions. Crop-specific climate now applies once; storage remains bounded. Craft labor allocation was also adjusted so adding recipes did not starve essential toolmaking of all workshop time. Tubers use wet-mass yields and retain lower food-energy density. A later 500-year check isolated excessive fixation energy costs as the cause of nitrogen exhaustion. A matched century-long continuation grew to 5,639 people with the calibrated cost, versus 1,346 at the old cost; tool recycling alone reached 1,375. Nitrogen remains a recorded atmospheric input, and fixation still consumes new energy. These changes preserve island resource scarcity rather than applying a general fertility multiplier.

Performance work first cached GPU history pipelines and reusable buffers. A matched seed-17 30-year comparison fell from 18.74 to 10.27 seconds with identical annual samples and cultural/agricultural output. These are local observed timings, not universal hardware guarantees. Longer histories exposed repeated genealogy-validation scans; temporary indexed lookups retain the same ancestry and marriage checks. `history_profile` benchmarks a saved continuation and records a deterministic history fingerprint, stage times, record counts, and peak host memory.

A follow-up mature-world event-archive benchmark, with exact matching history fingerprints, reduced a two-year continuation from 2.04 to 1.57 seconds. Transaction setup fell from 217 to 39 ms; failure restores the original event prefix. See `output/patron-clone-before2.json` and `output/patron-clone-after2.json`.

See [the seed evaluation reports](patron-results.md) for seed and control results. Archive metadata remains bounded, with a 256 MiB limit for extended histories.

Independent archive audits can be run with `python3 scripts/patron_audit.py output/patron-complete.17.world`. `cargo run --release --example history_replay -- WORLD REPORT.json` checks a two-year mature continuation against monthly advancement with an intermediate checkpoint, including bitwise GPU field comparisons.
