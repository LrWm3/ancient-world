# Integrated development worklist

This tracks the eight areas authorized after the essential-service recovery update.
A diagnostic or first implementation does not close a larger structural gap.
Completed increments retain tests, evidence and commits; unresolved work stays listed.

| Area | Status | Completion evidence still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Integrated calibration | Century ensembles and council-response correction implemented; interpretation continues | Retain published matched/held-out evidence; council concessions dominate sampled crises, so political balance remains an open calibration question |
| Cross-scale continuity | Source-level regional activation/retirement implemented; broader continuity open | Full local simulation and source provenance through transformed goods and deposits |
| Transport infrastructure | Endpoint and sea-port approach reservations plus [road upkeep](road-upkeep.md) and [labor-funded harbor work](harbor-work.md) implemented | Intermediate-edge reservations, spatial road works and year-round maintenance scheduling |
| Institutions and government | Explicit local offices, jurisdictions and finite terms; [institutional mandates and contested succession](institutional-succession.md) | Multi-site branches and multiple institution officer roles |
| Warfare and displacement | Finite occupation, provision-limited withdrawal and retained return itinerary implemented | Permanent units, sieges, resupply and reinforcement; civilian journeys already conserve households |
| Economic actors | Industry-linked income plus household-owned workshop operators, finite operating accounts and closure implemented; [four-seed matched evaluation completed](workshop-operator-calibration.md) | Private physical factory ownership, input/output trading by firms, household labor matching, creditors and multi-owner shares |
| Wildlife differentiation | Open; founder assembly and dispersal implemented | Persistent ecotypes, adaptation and species identities |
| Playable historical places | Regional site/object snapshots and work-funded canonical recovery implemented | Active local simulation, excavation geometry and dated burial layers |

The first evaluation measures current integrated behavior. It is game-balance
assessment, not empirical calibration against historical observations. Annual
samples cannot establish monthly maxima or prove an absent mechanism was exercised.
