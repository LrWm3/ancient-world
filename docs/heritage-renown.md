# Heritage renown

A successful return creates one dated recognition record linked
to its optional artifact, expedition, returning named crew, sponsoring civilization,
institution and tradition. The received-fragment event references these actors.
Repeated delivery cannot create a second object or award. Older archives default
to no recognition records; prior voyages are not retroactively awarded fame.

## Witnesses and bounded effects

Recognition begins at the receiving port. During cultural Respond, completed
commerce of at least one kilogram on a settlement pair in the current month
provides a contact opportunity. Land and sea deliveries both count; an unused
route does not. Only witnesses who knew the achievement before this month can
transmit, and recipients are gathered before applying changes. Repeated calls
cannot spread a second hop in the same month. Abandoned endpoints do not transmit.

This is a traffic-supported social contact rule, not news carried in a particular
cargo hold: the prior completed witness snapshot supplies the news, without
reconstructing what the crew knew at dispatch. A completed delivery still counts
if its route subsequently closes. Each observer retains its first-receipt date.

Weight is the returning fraction of the crew times the objective weight,
decaying exponentially with a 240-month half-life. Late news does not refresh the achievement.
Combined recognition saturates as total / (1 + total); it cannot grow without
bound. These are intentionally modest game weights, not empirical prestige units.

## Connections

- Among operational institutions that already meet the full sponsorship funding
  threshold, locally known heritage success breaks ties within the preferred
  institutional type. Renown cannot finance an expedition or bypass recruitment.
- Returning named crew receive up to 0.30 additional council-candidate score where
  their achievement is known. Existing faction membership, eligibility and support
  requirements remain. Fame does not add skill or surviving crew.
- Known heritage adds up to 0.15 relative religious appeal in the existing
  sustained-persuasion model. Both incumbent and alternative traditions receive
  credit, so the signal can support retention as well as attraction. Contact,
  material concerns, doctrine and persuasion thresholds still apply.
- A known same-tradition artifact can attract an existing funded pilgrimage to its
  current site. It requires an accessible object, an operational religious or
  scholarly institution, a passable route and sufficient travel work. Existing
  food reserves, offerings, travel costs and learning limits remain. Departure
  events link the visit to the object and its recovery.
- A publicly funded recovery adds at most 0.01 local administrative loyalty,
  scaled by crew survival, only if the paying civilization still governs the port.
  Private and institutional successes do not automatically award this ruler credit.

Discovery recognition persists separately from custody. Loss or destruction removes
the pilgrimage destination, without erasing the crew's recovery. An object moved
to another accessible institution carries its destination benefit. No renown awards
are generated by repeated display or study, and there is no automatic donation,
global diplomatic advantage, religious conversion or supernatural confirmation.

## Limits and tests

This first pass reuses pilgrims rather than introducing a separate scholarly
visitor population. Paid heritage studies still produce their existing records and
interpretations; they do not yet renew publicity. Institution selection is the
expedition-support connection; renown does not yet influence individual volunteers.
The record exposes civilization-level recognition, but cross-border diplomatic
prestige has no additional dedicated consumer.

Tests exercise local versus unknown reputation, age decay, duplicate delivery,
one-edge diffusion, same-month repeat calls, closed routes, custody loss and
serialized continuation. Existing religion and pilgrimage tests check compatibility
with persistent persuasion and finite travel costs. Generated test logs belong
under ignored output/.

## Verification

The final heritage suite passed both tests with GPU execution enabled, including
production delivery, public-versus-private government credit, duplicate protection,
contact propagation, custody loss and serialized continuation. Five religious
dynamics tests and the finite-cost pilgrimage/recovery integration test also passed.
Formatting, all-target Clippy with warnings denied and repository artifact checks
passed.

Commands: cargo test --lib heritage; cargo test --lib culture::dynamics;
cargo test --lib pilgrimage. Each test command used
-- --include-ignored --test-threads=1. These are controlled mechanism and regression
checks, not a multi-century calibration of prestige or religious outcomes.
