# Civilization history: current guide

The simulated island civilizations establish permanent settlements only on the
central islands by design. Permanent colonization of the Ancient World by
these civilizations is not a project goal. Expeditions and temporary camps provide
contact with the Ancient World within that setting rule.

Whether the Ancient World should host civilizations of its own remains under
discussion. They are not implemented, but neither are they a committed feature or
an unfinished requirement.

History uses monthly resource and population accounting, with sparse CPU records
for people, institutions, politics and events. Individual local gameplay is not
implemented.

## Remaining structural limits

- Regional surveys are derived snapshots, not editable local simulations with
  authoritative activation/retirement contracts.
- Mineral-specific processing exists, but complete source provenance through mixed
  products, household purchases, waste burial and archaeological recovery does not.
- Institutional branches, first-class general offices, persistent garrisons and
  multi-stage siege warfare remain extensions to current regional abstractions.
- Dated reports influence destination preferences; admission still uses current
  capacity safeguards. Reciprocity is assistance in kind, not a general loan market.
- Heritage finds are finite fictional caches, not evidence generated by a simulated
  ancient civilization. Patron reunions and major revelations are excluded.
- Empirical calibration, broader resolution/hardware studies and held-out validation
  remain separate from functional tests and game-balance seed runs.

These are limitations, not a promised delivery sequence. Original milestone plans
and the version-one food-proxy guide are retained in the [design archive](archive/README.md).
