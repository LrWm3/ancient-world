# Institutional operating capacity

Institutions have an abstract readiness score supported by local members, finite quarterly work, money and usable meeting space. The current building model is described in [material objects and expandable facilities](material-objects-and-facilities.md).

New bundled worlds choose affordable structural and roofing methods and can expand their institutions room by room. There is no single default building mass. Small facilities can finish during the initial founding allowance; larger ones accumulate work. Existing completed rooms can remain usable during an extension. Repairs consume the actual component materials, while replaced material enters existing waste and C/N/P ledgers.

## Organizational support

New institutions start at 50% readiness. Two locally present adult members provide full staffing. Administrative work needs 0.025 worker-months per quarter and upkeep costs 0.5 abstract money, transferred from institution to town. Construction and repair reserve additional work from the same limited settlement cultural allocation. Remote members and people on journeys do not supply local labor.

Support is capped by staffing, paid upkeep, administrative work and usable space relative to institutional demand. Readiness changes quarterly by `+0.12 × support − 0.08 × (1 − support) − 0.12 × disruption`, bounded to 0–1. This is a game rule, not measured organizational physiology.

Below 25% readiness, institutional lessons and merchant-house expedition sponsorship stop. An impairment notice records the crossing; recovery is announced at 60% to avoid repeated threshold notices. Individual teaching and other expedition sponsors retain their own requirements. Low capacity preserves institutional identity, knowledge and property.

See the opt-in [operating-budget pilot](institution-operating-budgets.md) for dated,
work-dependent funding requests and their limits. Funding does not itself remove
the usable-space requirement.

## Compatibility

Archives without capacity state retain their original rules. Archives with old meeting places retain their embodied materials: either the early two-kg token foundation or the later fixed 2,000-kg hall. They receive no free materials or rewritten historical construction events.

The old two-kg record repairs against two kg and 0.1 worker-month per complete replacement. The old 2,000-kg hall repairs against its actual mass and four worker-months per replacement. Those legacy structures retain their prior readiness, condition and construction clocks. All saves persist at existing history boundaries.

Inactive institutions retain their final condition; abandoned institutional ruins are not independently weathered in this version. Geometry, shared rental space and detailed building engineering remain outside this implementation.

## Checks

Hardware-backed capacity fixtures verify legacy cash, work and repair-material accounting, local staffing, neglect/recovery, old archive defaults and construction continuation. New facility tests verify substitution, variable scale, paid expansions, component repairs and continuation; the multi-seed report and commands are in the linked document. These are implementation checks and game-behavior observations, not scientific calibration.
