# Heritage visitor access

A recovered object's reputation and its current accessibility are separate.
Witnesses retain the remembered expedition achievement after loss, destruction or
custody changes. Visits now require more than a scholarly/religious institution
somewhere in the same town.

`heritage_renown::destination` requires an operational local host with its leader
present, and any named physical custodian also present. Community objects need a
host in their owning community; institutional objects need their owning
institution; privately owned objects need a present owner who leads or belongs to
the host. Competing ownership claims do not move the physical object. Existing
recognition, faith, route, travel-work and occupied-site gates still apply.

This is a simple access convention, not a consent negotiation or a staffed visitor
service. It does not charge destination room-time, create donations, confer new
ownership, or change manuscript access and interpretation rules. Those are broader
connections. A visitor may still choose an ordinary sacred destination through the
existing pilgrimage fallback when no accessible heritage destination qualifies.

The recovery/study fixture checks owning versus unrelated institutions, local
versus absent private owners and custodians, community ownership, missing host
leadership, lost objects, preserved achievement and serialized rumor continuation.
Run `cargo test --lib heritage_study_requires_access_work_and_preserves_prior_readings -- --ignored`.
