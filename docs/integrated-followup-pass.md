## Longer balance result

The isolated corrected council ensemble completes all six 200-year histories for
seeds 17, 81 and 1024. Cash-gap support reduces cumulative unpaid administrative
cost from 9.97–15.41% to 4.63–5.38%. Ending population improves in one seed and falls
in two; active-town counts fall in all three treatments. Final-decade population
change improves, but that alone does not prove stability. Physical shortages and
purchasing gaps both occur. The policy remains opt-in.

All runs retain zero population residual, food residual below 1.584e-6 and ending
economic residuals below 2.395e-5 in reported native units. Both strict matched-report
comparators pass. Actual wars and expeditions are absent from these histories;
separate funded fixtures cover those relevant paths without claiming ensemble
coverage. Read the stress report for original failures, corrected-source provenance
and sensitivity of long histories to the allocation rounding change.
