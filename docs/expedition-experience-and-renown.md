# Expedition experience and recognition

This is game tuning, not a historical model. Successful returns with confirmed
observations can earn local recognition without creating an artifact. A physical
recovery and its voyage do not earn duplicate awards. Failed trips and empty
rescues earn no successful-return award.

Awards use surviving participation and an objective weight: ordinary research
0.35, successful rescue 0.65, patron searches/inscriptions/old literature 1.0.
Witnessed recognition decays exponentially with a 240-month half-life. Hearing
old news does not restart that clock. Total recognition still saturates and
travels through existing contact; it is not globally available knowledge.
Personal leadership selection uses a 0.30 recognition weight; faction appeal
retains its separate weight.

Existing saved artifact recognitions retain their full-strength baseline.
New artifact-free awards do not create pilgrimage destinations or objects to
own, sell or study. They preserve the founding affiliation as an origin link,
not a new theology.

Experienced teams retain all baseline archaeological opportunities. In the
remaining two fifths of endpoints, research competence permits an additional
deterministic chance, bounded at 75% for maximum competence. No repeated survey
rolls, additional objects per endpoint, or bypass of finite material accounting
are introduced.

## Workshop experience pilot

Named workshop participants contribute a grant-weighted opening proficiency,
using completed practice in the recipe family and existing peer learning.
Maximum proficiency adds 75% equivalent productive capacity per paid month.
Untrained, aggregate and legacy-zero inputs retain normal productivity.

The GPU discounts only contracted operator time, not communal or household
labor. Installed equipment, orders, recipe inputs and storage still cap output.
Actual time is returned in the existing enterprise-used ledger and settles personal
commitments and learning; productive-equivalent capacity is not counted as
additional time or wages. This pilot does not yet multiply agricultural, military,
caregiving or other occupational effort.

## Verification

The deterministic seed-17 sweep across 10,000 cell keys found 6,109 baseline
opportunities, 7,531 at half competence, and 9,028 at maximum competence.
Every baseline success remained available at higher competence. These are
opportunity counts, not guaranteed objects delivered home.

The heritage integration fixture checks artifact-free recognition, repeated-return
idempotence, a failed-rescue negative control, successful-rescue credit, physical
object counts, record validation, contact propagation and finite artifact study.
The 240- and 480-month checks give exactly one-half and one-quarter weight.

The named-work GPU comparison changes only completed practice between saved
histories. Both receive the same paid time allowance; veterans meet the same
order-limited output with more unused total craft time. This fixture is demand
limited, so it does not claim higher total production. Completed operator time
remains within reservations and economy residuals remain within the fixture's
0.02 tolerance. The existing funded/unfunded workshop and checkpoint-continuation
fixture also passed, as did all three leadership fixtures.

Long-history balance and experience effects outside workshops remain untested
by this increment. The repository-wide constants cleanup remains in progress;
the separate first commit only advances the owning-file checklist.
